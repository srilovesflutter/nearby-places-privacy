1. Who we are

Nearby Places is a Flutter Android application published by Bollojula Srikanth (package name com.srikanth.nearbyplaces). The app helps you discover tourist and other places of interest near your current location, view details about them, and open or navigate to a place in Google Maps.

The app does not require an account and is not connected to a Nearby Places backend server that stores your personal information.

2. Information we collect

The app requests access to your device’s location while you are using the app (foreground only). Location is used solely to find places near you and to sort or display them by distance. The app does not track your location in the background.

When you search for nearby places, the app may send your approximate coordinates to the third-party services listed below so they can return nearby places and related information.

The app does not:

  • Create or require user accounts
  • Show advertisements
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or payment information through the app
  • Sell your personal information

3. How we use information

Location information is used only to:

  • Look up nearby tourist and other places of interest
  • Show you details about a selected place
  • Build a link so you can view a place, or start navigation to it, in Google Maps if you choose to do so

We do not use location or any other information for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties except as needed to provide the features you request, as follows:

  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API and Nominatim: the app may send approximate coordinates (and related map queries) to retrieve nearby places and place details from OpenStreetMap data.
  • Wikipedia APIs: the app may send approximate coordinates or place identifiers to retrieve publicly available descriptions or related information about a place.
  • Google Maps: if you tap an option to open a location or navigate there, the app opens Google Maps on your device using a link that includes the place or coordinates you selected. Google’s own terms and privacy policy then apply to your use of Google Maps.

These services are operated by third parties. They may receive the information contained in the request (such as approximate coordinates) and process it under their own privacy policies. We do not operate those services and do not control how they independently handle data.

Apart from the API requests and Maps links described above, the app does not share your data with third parties.

5. Storage and retention

The publisher does not store or sell your personal data on any server. The app has no Nearby Places account system and no publisher-operated cloud database of user profiles or location history.

Place results may be held temporarily on your device while you use the app. The app does not keep a long-term record of your location on our servers, because we do not operate such servers for this app.

Your device and the third-party services named in this policy may retain information according to their own settings and policies (for example, Android permission and system logs, or logs kept by OpenStreetMap or Wikipedia operators).

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit what the app accesses and transmits. Location is requested only in the foreground, and only approximate coordinates are sent to the place-lookup services described above. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data in transit to third-party APIs.

7. Children’s privacy

Nearby Places is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at seven.srikanth@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete that information if it is within our control.

8. Your choices

You can deny or revoke the app’s location permission at any time in your Android settings. If location permission is not granted, the app cannot look up places based on your current location.

You can also choose not to open Google Maps from the app. Opening Maps or starting navigation happens only when you tap those options.

You may uninstall the app at any time to stop all further use of location by Nearby Places.

If you have a question or a request about privacy, including any rights that may apply under the law where you live, contact seven.srikanth@gmail.com.

9. Third-party services

When the app contacts a third-party service, or when you leave the app to open Google Maps, that service’s privacy policy applies to information it receives. For reference:

  • OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy
  • Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted at this page with a revised effective date. If changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, such as updating the policy URL used in the Google Play Store listing.
